Chapter 3 · Energy storage

When the grid goes dark, keep the lines running.

A grid-tied solar system switches off during an outage, for safety. A battery energy storage system (ESS) changes that. It stores solar and off-peak power, keeps critical loads alive through load shedding, and can supply your evening peak so you buy less at the most expensive hours.

Backup through load shedding

Critical machines, cold rooms, servers and lifts stay on. Less diesel, less noise, fewer stoppages.

Peak shaving

Charge from solar or off-peak power, discharge in the evening peak, and flatten your demand.

More solar, used

Store midday surplus instead of exporting it, and use your own clean power after sunset.

Who this is for
  • Factories with sensitive machines
  • Hospitals & clinics
  • Data rooms & telecom sites
  • Cold storage & agro-processing
  • Homes with frequent outages

Questions

Straight answers.

Can my factory get solar with no upfront investment?

Yes, where the site qualifies. Under the investment and power purchase agreement (PPA) model, the solar plant is financed and built on your roof and you pay only for the electricity it produces, at an agreed per-unit rate. The rate and term are set per project after a site survey.

How big can a rooftop solar system be under net metering in Bangladesh?

Under the Net Metering Guidelines 2025, rooftop solar can be sized up to 100% of your sanctioned load, up from 70% under the 2018 guidelines. We check the current rules and your utility's process during the survey.

Does solar keep running during load shedding?

A grid-tied solar system switches off when the grid goes down, for safety. To keep running through load shedding you need a battery energy storage system (ESS) or a hybrid design, which we size to the loads you want to protect.

What is EraseAI Firewall?

EraseAI Firewall, made by Vantward Solutions Pte. Ltd. in Singapore, checks what staff are about to send to ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini and holds the message if it finds personal data, card numbers, passwords or API keys. The Chrome extension is free; team plans add an admin dashboard and audit log. Future Onward OPC rolls it out for organisations in Bangladesh.

Which areas of Bangladesh do you serve?

We are based in Dhaka and work on projects across Bangladesh, including industrial zones and EPZs. Send us your location and we will confirm a site visit.
