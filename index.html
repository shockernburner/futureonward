Software

We develop bespoke software applications to streamline operations, enhance efficiency, and provide actionable insights. Our key offerings include:

  • Customized ERP: Flexible ERP with drag-and-drop functionality and a powerful dashboard creator leveraging LLMs for user-prompted customization.
  • EV Charging Station ERP & Mobile Apps: Planning, operation, and maintenance management for EV charging infrastructure.
  • Content Management System (CMS): Archiving, indexing, and search across large document and media collections.
  • Commercial Credit Scoring Prediction System: Data-driven models for accurate credit risk assessment.
  • Document & Media Archive, Index, and Search: Efficient storage, organization, and retrieval at scale.
  • Inventory Management System: Real-time tracking and control across industries.
  • EdTech Platform: Self-paced learning with engaging, personalized content.
  • Flying Mission Planner & Map Creation Tools: Software for drone/aerial operations enabling precise mission planning and detailed map generation.
